# 1. MVP scope

## 1.1 Platforms

1.1.1 Wordoken runs as a PWA in mobile and desktop browsers.

## 1.2 Included features

1.2.1 Players form words from supplied letters, including every required letter.

1.2.2 Timed games last up to five minutes.

1.2.3 Accepted timed-game words add points and trigger character attacks.

1.2.4 Players revisit past puzzles for untimed practice.

1.2.5 Timed games award XP and coins.

1.2.6 Coins buy avatar items, chests, and game packs.

1.2.7 Money buys coins and subscriptions.

1.2.8 The app displays ads.

1.2.9 The app includes a tutorial, music, sound effects, and volume controls.

1.2.10 Players choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.

1.2.11 Players compete against recorded opponents.

1.2.12 Players share challenge links to their completed games.

1.2.13 Players can play as guests or sign up and sign in.

1.2.14 Only registered players can edit usernames and avatars.

1.2.15 Higher levels unlock more avatar items.

1.2.16 Subscribers can access subscriber-only avatar items.

1.2.17 Cached games can be played offline.

1.2.18 Players can reset or change their passwords.

1.2.19 Using every letter earns a Wordoken bonus.

1.2.20 Characters can knock out opponents in Medium and Hard games.

## 1.3 Deferred releases

1.3.1 Native iOS and Android releases follow the MVP.
